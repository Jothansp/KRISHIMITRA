Training data
==============
Place your REAL historical dataset here as:

historical_weather.csv

Required columns:
date,rainfall_mm,temperature_c,humidity,pressure_hpa,river_level,soil_moisture,slope,flood_risk

flood_risk must contain LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH based on real historical flood/event records.

historical_weather_demo.csv is synthetic data only for testing the application. Do not
use its model metrics or predictions as real-world evidence in the final report.
